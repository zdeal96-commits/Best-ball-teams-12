Golf Best Ball Sheet with Puter

This version follows the sheet order from top to bottom. It treats each row as one best ball matchup between two teams, not as holes. It fills rows the AI is confident about, keeps unclear rows in place for you to review, supports uneven team sizes like 2v3, supports multiple bet sheet images, and now gives Google, Claude, and GPT repeated full image, top focus, bottom focus, and audit passes. Claude and GPT also fall back to transcript audit mode when Puter direct image vision is unavailable.
