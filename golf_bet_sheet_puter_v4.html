Upload a pre round Squabbit CSV, a completed round CSV, and a bet sheet image. This version uses Puter.js in the browser instead of your own backend. It can OCR the bet sheet, ask AI to extract bets, let you review and edit them, calculate settlements for supported bet types, and export a full PDF plus one PDF per player.
